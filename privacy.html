Rewilded

Privacy policy · Effective November 12, 2026

Rewilded ("the app") does not collect, sell, or share personal data. The developer has no servers and receives no information about you or how you play.

Your progress

Your progress, best results and sound setting are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on, your progress and best results are also saved to your own iCloud account, so they come back if you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone. That copy is held by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the developer cannot see it. You can turn it off under iCloud in the Settings app.

Purchases

The Endless Blocks purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your payment details or Apple Account; the app only learns from Apple whether the purchase is owned.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your score (the furthest block you have finished and the number of moves you used) is sent to Apple's Game Center so it can appear on the leaderboard, together with your Game Center name. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and you can turn Game Center off in the Settings app. The developer only sees what the leaderboard shows publicly.

No tracking, ads, or analytics

The app contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking code.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children.
